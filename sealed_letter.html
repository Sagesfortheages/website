📯 The Sealed Letter

You are a courier of the old roads.
A sealed letter has been entrusted to you. The address is torn away — all that remains is the name of the great work its reader wrote. No one knows the author's name, but the innkeeper, the ferryman, and the bookseller each recall one fact about him. Gather when, where, and what he wrote, then name him at the study hall and deliver the letter. You carry three wax seals; every wrong answer cracks one — but asking the locals for hints is free.
