🗝️ The Locked Study

Night falls in the old study hall — and the door has locked behind you.
This study belonged to a great sage, and it answers to its master's name alone. Four objects in the room hide puzzles: solve them to win four brass keys — and with each key, learn one fact about the master. Turn all four in the door, name the sage, and walk free. But the candle is burning: wrong answers make it gutter, and when the flame dies you are locked in until morning.
