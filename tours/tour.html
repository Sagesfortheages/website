Rashi Opens the Door

Rashi, the sage of Troyes, became the foundational commentator for learning Chumash. His concise explanations and careful selection of midrash illuminate the simple meaning of the text and open the door for generations of learners.

The words of Rashi are like clear water — everyone can drink and understand.
